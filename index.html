Meet Joel

The artist behind Sleepy Tattoos

Hey, I'm Joel, the artist behind Sleepy Tattoos. Raised in Westchester County, I've been turning ideas into permanent works of art for over 20 years. I specialize in black and gray realism, custom lettering, and large-scale portrait pieces, bringing every detail to life with precision, creativity, and passion.

For me, tattooing is more than just ink on skin. It's about connecting with people, hearing their stories, and creating something meaningful that represents who they are, where they've been, and what matters most to them.

Whether it's a tribute to someone special, a bold statement piece, or an idea you've been carrying for years, my goal is to bring your vision to life and create something you'll be proud to wear forever.

Your story. Your vision. My art.

Let's turn your ideas into something unforgettable.

— Joel · Sleepy Tattoos

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I book a tattoo with Joel?

Appointments are booked by text. Text 914-409-5271 with your idea, the placement and any reference photos, and Joel will take it from there. You can also DM @s13epy on Instagram.

What are your hours?

Monday to Saturday, 11 AM to 8 PM. The shop is closed on Sunday. Appointments are booked by text at 914-409-5271.

Do I need to be 18 to get tattooed?

Yes. Sleepy Tattoos works with clients 18 and over only, and a valid ID is required.

What tattoo styles does Joel specialize in?

Black and grey realism, portraits of loved ones, icons and pets, custom lettering and cover-ups. Joel has been tattooing for over 20 years.

Can you cover up an old tattoo?

Yes. Old ink, faded work or a piece that no longer fits your story can be reworked into a new design. Text a photo of the tattoo you want covered to 914-409-5271.

Where is Sleepy Tattoos?

375 Central Ave, White Plains, NY 10606. Appointments are booked by text at 914-409-5271.
